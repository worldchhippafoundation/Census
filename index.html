About World Chhippa Foundation

This portal is exclusively created for the global census and population count of the traditional Chhippa Muslim Community (originating from Marwar, Ahmedabad, Chittor, and associated regions, identified by ancestral sub-castes/clans like Tak, Molani, Bhati, etc.).

Important Notice: This foundation does not collect any donations or funds, nor does it host political news or community bulletins. It is strictly and purely a demographic census directory.
